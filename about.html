Yiran Shu
About · Dance Film · Choreography · Modeling/Acting · Violin · Press · Contact

Yiran Shu is a multidisciplinary performing artist whose work spans dance, violin performance, and movement-based filmmaking.

She finds common ground across cultures, believing that deeply personal experiences can reveal universal emotions. Through her work, she seeks to give form to what is left unspoken.

Professional Highlights & Recognition

  • Media Features: Featured in Billboard, Dance Enthusiast, and other publications for her choreographic and performance work.
  • Awards & Film Selections: Award-winning creator of A Quiet Longing (Los Angeles Short Film Awards), official selection at CineAsian Film Festival.
  • Leadership: Founder and Director of Summer Breeze.
  • Education & Background: NYU Tisch alumna bridging performance and creative technology.
