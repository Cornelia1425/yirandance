About · Choreography · Modeling · Dance film · Acting · Contact

I am Yiran Shu — a choreographer, performer, and filmmaker working between slow images, physical detail, and the stories bodies carry when words run out.

My practice moves across stage, camera, and collaboration: choreography for live and lens, dance film, and roles where movement and character meet. I care about nuance, rhythm, and the courage it takes to stay with a small gesture until it becomes legible.
