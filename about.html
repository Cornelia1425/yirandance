Yiran Shu
About · Dance Film · Choreography · Modeling/Acting · Violin · Press · Contact

Yiran Shu is a multidisciplinary performing artist whose work spans dance, violin performance, and movement-based filmmaking.

She finds common ground across cultures, believing that deeply personal experiences can reveal universal emotions. Through her work, she seeks to give form to what is left unspoken.
