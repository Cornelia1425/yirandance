About

I am a dancer, choreographer, and movement artist based in New York.

My work moves between stage, film, and still image. I am interested in how small gestures can hold a long story, and how the body can stay honest in front of a camera.

Practice

Choreography · movement direction · performance

I work on dance for film, live choreography with musicians and artists, and movement for fashion and image-making. Collaboration is central to what I do.
